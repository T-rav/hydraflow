# Architecture Console

**Chair:** senior-principal · **Seats:** vp-eng (is the justification demonstrated or claimed), product-manager (sequencing + migration cost) · **Inherits:** [the general contract](README.md).

## Convenes for

Proposed ADRs; new ports/loops (registry rows); standards changes; ADR-loop findings (touchpoint drift, conformance failures) that need a ruling rather than a mechanical fix. Advisory only on kernel standards and `factory_autonomy/policy.yaml` — **the operator ratifies**; break-glass is never a chamber's to grant.

## Procedure

1. Chair examines structure at the boundaries; proposes the minimal fix where one closes the gap.
2. VP-Eng attacks the justification; PM prices the consequences.
3. Chair consolidates: **ACCEPT / ACCEPT WITH FIX / REJECT / SUPERSEDE**, disagreements named.

## Bounds

The ADR machinery detects; this chamber adjudicates; `docs/adr/` stays the canonical artifact — records here capture the adjudication and link, never duplicate. An `enforced` classification the chamber cannot verify resolving is an automatic FIX. Records: [decisions/arch/](decisions/arch/) — committing the record is the chair's closing act.
